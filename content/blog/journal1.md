Ever gone through genuine game production? No? Me neither.
Oh, wait, you have..? That's... awkward...

Well, either way, this site documents my journey through my first professional,
collaborative game production journey as it unfolds throughout the entire school year.
Each week I will upload a devlog covering development progress and my contributions to the game from that week.

"But, Kat," you ask, "what _is_ the game?"

All we have at this very moment is a basic premise: a wave defense game where your life is expendable,
but the life of a very important NPC, however, is not.
And this NPC... well... it has zero survival instinct.
Your job is to keep it alive through five waves.
Sounds simple, but here's the catch: after each wave,
players are forced to choose the NPCs next nerf to their behavior and navigation.
This could manifest in many forms:

- The NPC no longer listens when you command it to follow you.
- Every 60 seconds, the NPC picks a favorite spot for 30 seconds and refuses to leave.
- The NPC has less health.

My team is aiming for a cartoonish fantasy style that lends itself well to a comedy about how...
unaccustomed to surviving this NPC is.
We'll be using Godot 2D, for now at least.
We're trying to start small for our minimum-viable product.
With any luck, we'll find the fun and go from there.

That about wraps it up.
See you next week!
